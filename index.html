Welcome to POND 2026

The POND 2026 meeting aims to gather the research community working on disease progression models and their applications in neurodegenerative disorders and beyond.

Our goal is to bring the community together for this two-day event to share the latest advances in the field and discuss the future of disease progression modelling.

    Sophia Antipolis

    Plan your visit

    The Inria Center at Université Côte d’Azur is located in the Sophia Antipolis technology park in Valbonne.

    Hotels and residences are available near the Inria Center. We recommend booking accommodation in advance.

    If you are travelling by plane, the nearest airport is Nice Côte d’Azur. From the airport, take a shuttle or bus to Sophia Antipolis and alight at the “Inria” stop.

    By car, follow the A8 motorway and take the exit towards Antibes–Grasse–Sophia Antipolis when arriving from Marseille or Aix, or Antibes–Sophia Antipolis when arriving from Nice. Continue towards Sophia Antipolis and Route des Lucioles.
